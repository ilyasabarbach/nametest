Frequently Asked Questions

Q: The game is stuck on the loading screen. What should I do?

A: Please ensure you have a stable internet connection. Try closing the Mini App (tap the X) and reopening it from the bot menu.

Q: My quiz result won't share to my friends.

A: Ensure you are using the latest version of the Telegram app. Sharing requires "Inline Mode" to be enabled on your device.

Q: How do I restart a quiz?

A: You can always navigate back to the Home screen from the result page to try the quiz again or choose a different one.
